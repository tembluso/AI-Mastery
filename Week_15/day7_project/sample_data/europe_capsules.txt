France: Paris is the capital and largest city. The Eiffel Tower is a landmark located in Paris on the Champ de Mars.
Germany: Berlin is the capital. The Brandenburg Gate is a neoclassical monument in Berlin.
Italy: Rome is the capital. The Colosseum is an ancient amphitheater located just east of the Roman Forum.
Spain: Madrid is the capital city. The Prado Museum in Madrid holds one of the world's finest art collections.
Portugal: Lisbon is the capital. Belém Tower is a fortified tower located in the civil parish of Santa Maria de Belém in Lisbon.
United Kingdom: London is the capital. Big Ben is the nickname for the Great Bell of the striking clock at the north end of the Palace of Westminster in London.
