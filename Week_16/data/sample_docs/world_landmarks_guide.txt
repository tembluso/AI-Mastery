World Capitals & Landmarks — A Practical Factbook for RAG Testing
=================================================================

Overview
--------
This file is designed for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) demos. It contains concise,
fact-dense sections about countries, cities, landmarks, museums, rivers, mountains, food,
and brief historical notes. Each section aims to be specific enough that a semantic
retriever can surface the right chunk for a question. Use it to test chunking, top-k
retrieval, and reranking.


France
------
Capital: Paris. Currency: Euro. Language: French. Time zone: CET/CEST.
Rivers: Seine (flows through Paris), Loire, Garonne, Rhône.
Mountains: Mont Blanc (the highest in Western Europe, in the Alps near the Italian border).
Landmarks: Eiffel Tower (Paris, Champ de Mars), Arc de Triomphe (Place Charles de Gaulle),
Notre-Dame Cathedral (Île de la Cité), Palace of Versailles (Île-de-France).
Museums: The Louvre (world’s most visited), Musée d’Orsay (impressionist and post-impressionist),
Centre Pompidou (modern art). Cuisine highlights: baguette, croissant, crêpes, coq au vin,
ratatouille, crème brûlée. Events: Bastille Day (14 July) with a parade on the Champs-Élysées.
Notes: The Paris Métro is one of the world’s oldest rapid transit systems (opened 1900).

Germany
-------
Capital: Berlin. Currency: Euro. Language: German. Time zone: CET/CEST.
Rivers: Rhine (western border), Elbe (Hamburg & Dresden), Danube (Bavaria), Spree (Berlin).
Landmarks: Brandenburg Gate (Pariser Platz), Reichstag dome, Berlin TV Tower (Fernsehturm).
Museums: Museum Island includes the Pergamon Museum, Neues Museum, Altes Museum,
Bode Museum, Alte Nationalgalerie. Automotive hubs: Munich (BMW), Stuttgart (Mercedes-Benz & Porsche).
Events: Oktoberfest in Munich (late Sept–early Oct). Historic note: the Berlin Wall fell in 1989,
leading to German reunification in 1990.

Italy
-----
Capital: Rome. Currency: Euro. Language: Italian.
Landmarks: Colosseum (ancient amphitheater east of the Roman Forum), Pantheon (oculus),
Trevi Fountain, Vatican City (St. Peter’s Basilica, Sistine Chapel with Michelangelo frescoes).
Cities: Florence (Uffizi Gallery, Duomo), Venice (St. Mark’s Basilica, Grand Canal), Milan (Duomo, La Scala).
Food: pizza (Naples), pasta varieties (carbonara, bolognese), gelato, espresso. Rivers: Po (longest).
Mountains: Alps in the north; Apennines run the length of the peninsula.
Notes: The Leaning Tower of Pisa is a freestanding bell tower (campanile) with a tilt due to soft ground.

Spain
-----
Capital: Madrid. Currency: Euro. Language: Spanish (Castilian); co-official regional languages include Catalan,
Galician, and Basque. Museums: Prado Museum (Velázquez, Goya), Reina Sofía (Guernica by Picasso),
Thyssen-Bornemisza. Barcelona: Sagrada Família (Gaudí, still under construction), Park Güell, Casa Batlló.
Seville: Alcázar, Giralda, flamenco traditions. Valencia: City of Arts and Sciences by Santiago Calatrava.
Food: paella (Valencia), jamón ibérico, tortilla de patatas, churros with chocolate. Events: Running of the Bulls
in Pamplona during San Fermín (July). Football clubs: Real Madrid, FC Barcelona, Atlético de Madrid.

United Kingdom
--------------
Capital: London. Currency: Pound sterling. Language: English.
Rivers: Thames (London), Severn (longest in Great Britain). Landmarks: Big Ben (Great Bell of the
clock at the Palace of Westminster), Tower Bridge, Buckingham Palace, London Eye.
Museums: British Museum (Rosetta Stone), National Gallery (Trafalgar Square), Tate Modern (Bankside).
Universities: Oxford (oldest in English-speaking world), Cambridge, Imperial College London.
Regions: Scotland (Edinburgh Castle), Wales (Snowdonia), Northern Ireland (Giant’s Causeway).
Cultural notes: Afternoon tea, pub culture, Premier League football.

Portugal
--------
Capital: Lisbon. Currency: Euro. Language: Portuguese.
Landmarks: Belém Tower (UNESCO), Jerónimos Monastery, Praça do Comércio, 25 de Abril Bridge.
Porto: Dom Luís I Bridge, Ribeira district, port wine cellars in Vila Nova de Gaia.
Rivers: Tagus (Tejo) flows through Lisbon; Douro flows through Porto and the wine region.
Food: bacalhau (salt cod), pastéis de nata (custard tarts), caldo verde soup.
Music: Fado (especially in Lisbon and Coimbra).

Netherlands
-----------
Capital: Amsterdam. Currency: Euro. Language: Dutch.
Landmarks: Rijksmuseum (Rembrandt’s Night Watch), Van Gogh Museum, Anne Frank House, canals and narrow houses.
Other cities: Rotterdam (modern architecture, Erasmus Bridge), The Hague (International Court of Justice, Peace Palace).
Geography: Much of the country is below sea level; protected by dikes and polders. Flowers: tulips in Keukenhof gardens.
Transport: Bicycles are a dominant mode in cities; extensive cycling infrastructure.

Greece
------
Capital: Athens. Currency: Euro. Language: Greek.
Landmarks: Acropolis (Parthenon), Temple of Olympian Zeus, Ancient Agora.
Islands: Santorini (caldera, white-washed villages), Crete (Knossos), Mykonos (windmills, nightlife), Rhodes (medieval old town).
Food: moussaka, souvlaki, Greek salad (horiatiki), feta cheese, baklava. Seas: Aegean, Ionian, Mediterranean.
History: Birthplace of democracy and Western philosophy; major figures include Socrates, Plato, Aristotle.

Turkey
------
Capital: Ankara; largest city: Istanbul. Currency: Turkish lira. Language: Turkish.
Landmarks: Hagia Sophia (Byzantine basilica turned mosque/museum/mosque), Blue Mosque (Sultan Ahmed), Topkapı Palace.
Geography: Bosphorus Strait separating Europe and Asia; Cappadocia (tufa rock formations, hot air balloons).
Food: döner, kebab varieties, meze, baklava, Turkish tea and coffee. Ancient sites: Ephesus, Göbekli Tepe.
Notes: Grand Bazaar is among the world’s largest covered markets.

United States
-------------
Capital: Washington, D.C. Currency: U.S. dollar. Language: English (de facto).
Iconic cities: New York (Statue of Liberty, Empire State Building, Central Park, Broadway), Los Angeles (Hollywood, Griffith Observatory),
San Francisco (Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz), Chicago (Willis Tower, Millennium Park), Washington, D.C. (National Mall, Smithsonian museums).
Parks: Yellowstone (geysers), Grand Canyon, Yosemite, Zion.
Universities: Harvard, MIT, Stanford, Berkeley. Tech hubs: Silicon Valley, Seattle, Austin.
Food: regional diversity—barbecue (Texas, Kansas City), bagels (NYC), deep-dish pizza (Chicago), Cajun/Creole (New Orleans).

Japan
-----
Capital: Tokyo. Currency: Yen. Language: Japanese.
Landmarks: Tokyo Skytree, Shibuya Crossing, Meiji Shrine; Kyoto’s Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) and Fushimi Inari Taisha (torii gates);
Osaka Castle; Hiroshima Peace Memorial Park. Nature: Mount Fuji (iconic stratovolcano).
Food: sushi, ramen, tempura, okonomiyaki, wagyu beef; tea ceremony.
Transport: Shinkansen (bullet trains) with punctual high-speed service. Culture: anime, manga, festivals (matsuri), hanami (cherry blossom viewing).

China
-----
Capital: Beijing. Currency: Renminbi (yuan). Language: Mandarin Chinese.
Landmarks: Great Wall (sections near Beijing like Badaling and Mutianyu), Forbidden City (Palace Museum),
Temple of Heaven, Tiananmen Square. Shanghai: The Bund, Oriental Pearl Tower, Pudong skyline.
Xi’an: Terracotta Army of Qin Shi Huang. Rivers: Yangtze (longest in Asia), Yellow River (Huang He).
Food: diverse regional cuisines—Sichuan (spicy), Cantonese (dim sum), Shandong (seafood), Jiangsu (sweet-savory).
Inventions: papermaking, printing, compass, gunpowder.

India
-----
Capital: New Delhi. Currency: Indian rupee. Languages: Hindi & English (official at Union level) + many regional languages.
Landmarks: Taj Mahal (Agra), Qutub Minar and Red Fort (Delhi), Gateway of India (Mumbai), Hawa Mahal (Jaipur), Varanasi ghats on the Ganges.
Geography: Himalayas (highest range; Kanchenjunga is the highest peak in India), Thar Desert (Rajasthan).
Food: masala dosa, biryani, butter chicken, chaat, jalebi; diverse vegetarian traditions.
Festivals: Diwali (festival of lights), Holi (festival of colors), Durga Puja (Kolkata), Ganesh Chaturthi (Mumbai).

Egypt
-----
Capital: Cairo. Currency: Egyptian pound. Language: Arabic.
Landmarks: Pyramids of Giza and the Great Sphinx (on the Giza Plateau), Egyptian Museum (Tahrir Square),
Karnak Temple and Valley of the Kings (Luxor). River: Nile (flows north into the Mediterranean).
History: Ancient civilization known for hieroglyphics, pharaohs (e.g., Ramses II, Tutankhamun). Modern attraction: Nile cruises.
Cuisine: koshari (lentils, rice, pasta), ful medames (fava beans), taameya (Egyptian falafel).

Mexico
------
Capital: Mexico City. Currency: Mexican peso. Language: Spanish.
Landmarks: Teotihuacan (Pyramid of the Sun and Moon), Chichén Itzá (El Castillo), Palenque (Mayan ruins).
Museums: National Museum of Anthropology (world-class Mesoamerican collections). Food: tacos al pastor, mole, tamales, pozole.
Geography: Yucatán cenotes, Copper Canyon, Baja California peninsula. Art: Frida Kahlo, Diego Rivera.
Events: Day of the Dead (Día de Muertos) late Oct–Nov 2 with ofrendas and marigolds.

Brazil
------
Capital: Brasília. Currency: Brazilian real. Language: Portuguese.
Cities: Rio de Janeiro (Christ the Redeemer on Corcovado, Sugarloaf Mountain, Copacabana), São Paulo (economic center),
Salvador (Afro-Brazilian culture, Pelourinho). Nature: Amazon Rainforest and River (largest by discharge), Iguaçu Falls (on the border with Argentina).
Food: feijoada (black bean stew), pão de queijo (cheese bread), açaí bowls. Events: Carnival (Rio’s Sambadrome parades).

Rivers & Mountains (Cross-Region Quick Facts)
---------------------------------------------
- The Danube flows through or borders Germany, Austria, Slovakia, Hungary, Croatia, Serbia, Romania, Bulgaria, Moldova, and Ukraine; it empties into the Black Sea.
- The Rhine begins in the Swiss Alps, passes through Lake Constance, forms part of the Swiss–German and Franco–German border, and flows through Cologne to the North Sea.
- The Alps span France, Switzerland, Italy, Liechtenstein, Austria, Germany, and Slovenia; Mont Blanc (France/Italy) and the Matterhorn (Switzerland/Italy) are iconic.
- The Andes run along the western edge of South America, with Aconcagua (Argentina) as the highest outside Asia.
- The Himalayas include Mount Everest (Nepal/China border) and K2 in the Karakoram.
