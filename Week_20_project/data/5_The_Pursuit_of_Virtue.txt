The Pursuit of Virtue

Virtue is not a single act but a lifelong shaping of the soul. Aristotle viewed it as a habit—a mean between excess and deficiency. The Stoics saw it as alignment with nature. Scripture presents it as walking in righteousness before God. In every tradition, virtue stands as the bridge between potential and fulfillment. The pursuit of virtue is the pursuit of self-mastery, and through it, of peace.
